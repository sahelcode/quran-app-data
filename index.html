Privacy Policy

Hifz Quran · Neelgrove · Last updated: 6 September 2026
In short: Hifz Quran does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. The app works offline. An account is optional and is used only for the leaderboard.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers, or advertising IDs. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Your language, theme, and reading preferences
  • Last read page, bookmarks, and saved Mutashabihat pairs
  • Reading streak, reading time, and quiz history
  • Quran page images downloaded on first use

Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

3. Optional account (leaderboard)

The leaderboard is optional. The entire app works without an account. If you choose to create one, we use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (services of Google LLC) and store:

  • The display name you enter
  • Your email address, used only for sign-in and password reset
  • Your weekly and total reading minutes, and your streak
  • A randomly generated friend code

Other users of the leaderboard can see your display name, reading time, and streak. They cannot see your email address. We do not store profile photos.

4. Data we download

The app downloads Quran page images and Mutashabihat data from public GitHub repositories. These requests are anonymous. We do not receive any information about you from them.

5. Donations

Voluntary donations are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card or account information. Donations unlock no features — the app is fully free for everyone.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Deleting your data

To delete on-device data, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone settings. To delete your leaderboard account and all associated data, email us at the address below and we will remove it within 30 days.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
